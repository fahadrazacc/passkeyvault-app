Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Your vault is encrypted on your device with a key made from your master password. Nothing readable is sent anywhere. PassKey Vault has no accounts, no servers that hold your data, no ads and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: your logins, two-factor keys, cards, notes and settings, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your master password (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds) and exists only in memory while the vault is unlocked.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: if you turn it on, your master password is kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore, released only after a successful scan. It never leaves the phone.
  • Backups you make: encrypted backup files, saved wherever you choose. CSV exports you choose to create are not encrypted; delete them after use.
  • Before unlocking: a few non-secret settings, such as your theme, your optional password hint and the count of wrong unlock attempts.

The breach check

Only when you press "Check for breaches", PassKey Vault contacts Have I Been Pwned (api.pwnedpasswords.com). For each password it sends just the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash. The service replies with all leaked hashes starting with those characters, and the comparison happens on your device. Your passwords and their full hashes are never sent.

Your choices

  • Erase the vault on a device at any time in Settings → Erase vault.
  • Export your data in Settings → Export as CSV.
  • Turn Face ID or fingerprint unlock on or off in Settings.

Your master password

Your master password is never sent anywhere or stored as plain text. Because of this, nobody can reset it for you: if it's forgotten, the vault can't be opened.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub. Never include passwords or vault contents.
